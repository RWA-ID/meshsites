How to run a 3-tab demo

  1. Open three tabs: tab.html?name=A, ?name=B, ?name=C.
  2. In A: link B. In B: link C. (Don't link A–C.)
  3. In A: “Be the gateway”. In C: “Trust example.eth signer”.
  4. In C: Open example.eth. It arrives through B.
