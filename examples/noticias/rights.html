Staying informed when the network is down

  • Save what you need before you need it. Shelter lists, maps and phone numbers you saved while online keep working offline.
  • Write down key numbers on paper. A phone battery runs out; paper doesn't.
  • Save battery. Lower screen brightness, close apps you aren't using, and turn on low power mode. Keep Bluetooth on if you are sharing sites.
  • Agree on a meeting point with family in case you can't reach each other.
  • Check before you share. Prefer pages that show Verified, and note the time on any update: a frozen copy can be hours old.
  • Mind what you ask for. Asking nearby phones for a site lets them see which site you wanted.
